Muscle Recovery Anti-Aging

BPC-157

Body Protection Compound that accelerates healing of muscles, tendons, and gut tissue.

💉
Suggested Dosage
200–500 mcg per day, subcutaneous or intramuscular injection, split into 1–2 doses
Key Benefits
  • ✓ Accelerates tendon and ligament healing
  • ✓ Reduces inflammation in muscle tissue
  • ✓ Supports gut lining repair and leaky gut syndrome
  • ✓ Promotes angiogenesis (new blood vessel formation)
  • ✓ Neuroprotective effects supporting brain health
  • ✓ May reduce pain from injuries and chronic conditions
  • ✓ Supports joint health and cartilage repair
Research Notes
Derived from a protective protein found in human gastric juice
Multiple rodent studies show accelerated Achilles tendon healing vs. controls
Shown to upregulate growth hormone receptors in tendon fibroblasts
Research published in Journal of Physiology demonstrates gut cytoprotective effects
Currently in Phase II clinical trials for inflammatory bowel disease
